# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 2026

## Overview

ytm-shell is a native Windows client for YouTube Music. This privacy policy applies to the Discord Rich Presence integration feature.

## Data Collection

ytm-shell does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

## Discord Rich Presence

When the Discord Rich Presence integration is active, the following information is read locally from the YouTube Music player and sent directly to the Discord client running on your machine via Discord's local IPC protocol:

- Currently playing track title
- Artist name
- Playback state (playing or paused)

This data is sent exclusively to your local Discord client. It is never transmitted to any external server operated by this application. Discord's own privacy policy governs how Discord handles Rich Presence data.

## Third-Party Services

- **YouTube Music:** Content is streamed via YouTube Music's own web interface. All authentication and streaming is handled directly between your browser session and Google's servers. This application does not intercept, store, or process your YouTube account credentials or activity.
- **Discord:** Rich Presence data is sent to your local Discord client via IPC. See [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

## Analytics and Telemetry

This application contains no analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry of any kind.

## Contact

This is an open-source personal project. For questions or concerns, open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/goodguyjay/ytm-shell).
